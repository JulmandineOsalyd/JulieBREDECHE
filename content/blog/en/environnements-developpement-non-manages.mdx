On December 15, 2026, **unmanaged developer environments** will be suspended along with all their content. Apps, flows, agents and data become unavailable, for makers and for their users.

You have less than a month to find these environments and decide what happens to them.

---

## What changes: three dates to remember

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| **November 15, 2026** | End of creation: you can no longer create an unmanaged developer environment, including when signing up for the Power Apps Developer Plan |
| **December 15, 2026** | Suspension: existing environments are no longer accessible, along with their apps, flows, agents and data |
| **January 15, 2027** | Unmanaged developer environments are deleted |

Source: [Microsoft Learn, important changes coming to Power Platform](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/important-changes-coming#deprecation-of-unmanaged-developer-environments-and-the-power-apps-developer-plan).

---

## Two options to keep your flows and apps

For each unmanaged developer environment you still need, you have two paths **before December 15**.

| | **Option 1: move to a sandbox** | **Option 2: switch to a managed developer environment** |
|---|---|---|
| **Principle** | Export your solutions and import them into a "Sandbox" environment | Enable Managed Environments on the existing environment |
| **Pros** | No additional license required | Apps, flows, agents and data are kept without any migration |
| **Cons** | Solutions must be exported and imported manually between environments | Premium license required, only one managed developer environment per user |

Microsoft's principle is simple: as soon as an environment becomes managed, every user must [have a license that includes the right entitlements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/managed-environment-licensing): Power Apps Premium, Power Automate Premium, Copilot Studio and some Dynamics 365 licenses.

> **If you have premium licenses available, converting to a managed environment is the simplest path. Without a suitable license, moving your solutions to a sandbox is the only option.**

---

## Admin action plan in 3 steps

### 1. Identify

In the Power Platform admin center, open the list of environments. Filter on the "Developer" type, then "Managed = No". Use the "Last activity" column to see what is still in use.

### 2. Decide before December 15

For each environment still in use, choose one of the two options: **convert** it to a managed environment if the licenses are in place, or **move** the solutions to a sandbox.

### 3. Plan for what comes next

To give each maker a personal space, you can enable automatic environment routing on your tenant.

The goal is to keep makers autonomous without losing control. In a managed environment, the admin sees app usage and can set limits on sharing. It answers the [citizen developer governance challenges](/blog/enjeux-gouvernance-power-platform) I covered in a previous article.

---

## Maker action plan

If you build in a developer environment, three steps are enough:

- **Ask** your admin what is planned for your environment: conversion to a managed environment or a move to another environment
- **Package** your flows and Power Apps apps in solutions, if you have not done so already
- **Export** your solutions now to keep a copy of your work

---

## Why Microsoft is doing this

Unmanaged environments escape the controls that admins rely on: Managed Environments policies, usage insights and sharing limits.

Microsoft wants to give admins a consistent view of the whole tenant, while still leaving makers a personal space to build and test.

---

## My conclusion

This timeline leaves little room, but it is easy to follow if you act quickly. Three key points to remember:

1. **One date to remember: December 15**, because that is when content becomes inaccessible.
2. **The premium license determines whether you can convert**, and without it you have to migrate your solutions.
3. **Export your existing solutions as a safety net**, whatever strategy you choose.
